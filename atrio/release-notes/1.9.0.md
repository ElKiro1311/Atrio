Uninstalling without opening the store comes to EA, Amazon, itch and Xbox.

- **Uninstall without opening the store**, as Steam already did. Atrio asks you first, with the size of the game and its folder, and no store opens:
  - **EA:** it uses the uninstaller that the EA app leaves in Windows for each game.
  - **Amazon:** it uses its uninstaller for each game; Atrio accepts its second question for you, because you already said yes, and closes its final notice.
  - **itch:** it uninstalls with butler, the engine of the itch app.
  - **Xbox:** it removes the game with the same Windows command that Settings > Apps uses.
- If something fails, the store's usual uninstaller opens. Ubisoft and Epic still open their window: they have no way to uninstall without it.
- It follows the same setting: "Install and uninstall games without going through their store".
