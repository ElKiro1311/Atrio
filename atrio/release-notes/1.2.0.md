The first public version of Atrio.

- **All your stores in one place**, with every game listed once and you choosing where to play it from.
- **Collections** that you fill yourself or that fill themselves from a few filters, with their own page and their own shelf on Home.
- **Achievements and friends on the game page**, and a built-in browser for store links.
- **It updates itself.** Atrio lets you know about every new version, shows what's new and installs it with one click, with no account and without touching your library.
