Your week on Home, a Downloads section with each drive's storage, trailers with sound, backups of your saves, your captures on each game's page, your collections in Couch Mode, and the time you play outside Atrio counts too.

- **What you play outside Atrio counts:** if you open a game from its store, a shortcut or Steam, Atrio adds up the time it's in the foreground, as if you had opened it from Atrio. That way "Recent games" and your playtime come out right.
  - Programs that stay open all day without you using them, such as an animated wallpaper or a desktop pet, don't add hours.
  - If the store keeps its own count, as Steam does, its number wins at the next sync.
  - You can turn it off in Stores: "Count playtime for games you open outside Atrio".
- **Your week on Home:** this week's playtime compared with last week's, one column per day, the five games you've played the most and the achievements you've earned. It starts counting from this version.
- **A new Downloads section**, in the top bar:
  - **In progress:** whatever is being installed or uninstalled, from any store, with its progress.
  - **Storage:** each drive with its space (games, everything else and free) and your installed games sorted by size, with the last time you played each one.
  - Select several and uninstall them at once. Atrio first shows you the list, with each game's size and folder, and removes them one after another. If a store is left waiting, the rest stops, and "Stop" cuts the list short whenever you want.
- **Trailers with sound:** the "Trailer" button on a Steam game's page opens its store trailers. It uses the Microsoft Edge engine that comes with Windows, because Atrio's browser can't play their sound.
- **Backups of your saves:** when you close a game, Atrio backs up its saves with Ludusavi, an open-source program (MIT license) that knows where thousands of games keep their saves. Each game's page says when the last backup was made, and "Restore" brings it back, first backing up what you have in case you want to return; if that backup can't keep everything, nothing is touched. Even if you close Atrio right after playing, the backup is still made. It's off by default because it downloads Ludusavi (14 MB) from its official page: turn it on in Stores, "Back up your saves when you close a game".
- **Your collections in Couch Mode:** a "Collections" tab between Home and the filters, with one row per collection. The last card in each row opens the whole collection, and LB goes back to the collections.
- **Your captures on each game's page:** the ones from Steam, Xbox Game Bar, AMD, NVIDIA, Medal and Discord, with a viewer to go through them with the arrow keys. Clips open in your player. Atrio only looks in the folders on your PC where those programs keep them.
- **Uninstall from Ubisoft without touching its window:** Ubisoft Connect still opens its little window, but Atrio answers it for you, because you already said yes in Atrio, and closes its notice at the end.
- **Less memory while you play:** with a game running and Atrio minimized, Atrio frees the images it keeps in memory and loads them again when you come back. In testing, memory use went from about 420 MB down to almost nothing. You can turn it off in Stores: "Use less memory while you play".
