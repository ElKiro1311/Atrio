Switching games is now much faster and smoother.

- **The image changes instantly.** It comes in with the page in a single short fade, without showing the background first.
- **No stutter.** The window's blurred backgrounds are prepared once when they load instead of being recalculated every frame.
- **Your installed games, always ready.** Their images are saved in the background shortly after you open Atrio, and pages no longer have to wait for Steam.
