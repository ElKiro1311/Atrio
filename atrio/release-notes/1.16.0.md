Switching OptiScaler versions no longer drags along the settings of the one before, and what you change inside a game goes with you.

- **Each version starts from its own settings.** Until now, everything in the old OptiScaler.ini was copied over the new one. What only made sense in the previous version lingered, and could leave frame generation off, force a resolution or fall back to another upscaler, in that version and in the ones after it.
- **Your own settings go along.** What you change in OptiScaler's menu inside the game, or in OptiScaler.ini, is kept when you switch versions, also when you replace an OptiScaler installed by hand. A setting with another name in the new version is translated, and a value it doesn't have waits for a version that does.
  - The panel shows what you set inside the game, and **Your settings** lets you keep it or start over.
  - **Earlier settings** opens the copies of the files that were replaced or removed.
  - It counts from this version on. Where OptiScaler was installed by an earlier one, or from OptiScaler Client's own window, what you chose in the game for an option of the panel can't be told from what was installed: on the next switch the panel decides, and a copy of the file stays in **Earlier settings**.
- **OptiFG in the panel** (experimental): frame generation for games that don't have their own, with DirectX 12 and, on the nightly versions, DirectX 11 and Vulkan.
- **Fixed: automatic frame generation.** It took the files OptiScaler brings for the game's, and turned on something the game didn't have. Now it only looks at the game, and when there's nothing to work with it isn't offered.
- **Fixed: the HUD fix of OptiFG** was written where OptiScaler doesn't look for it.
- **Fixed: files left by an OptiScaler installed by hand.** Replacing a 0.9 version with a nightly one left its files next to the game, and removing the new one brought them back. The ones that came with it now go with it, and the panel names them before touching anything.
- **Fixed:** going back to the automatic upscaler left the previous one on.
- The quality levels say how much they upscale, and the hint of the option explains that any of them overrides the game's own setting.
- Nightly versions no longer get a separate fakenvapi, which they already have inside.
- Atrio's log keeps what each install, change and removal did.
- 421 tests.
