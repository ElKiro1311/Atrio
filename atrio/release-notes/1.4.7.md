Achievements closer at hand on each game's page, and a more compact Achievements tab.

- **"Show more" on the game page.** The achievements that don't fit unfold right there, below, without changing pages. With all of them in view, the same button folds them back.
- **Click an achievement to see what it's about**: whether you have it and since when, how to get it, how rare it is and, if it has them, its progress and points. Hidden ones spoil nothing until you press "Show it", and "Search for a guide" opens a search in Atrio's browser.
- **"Advanced view"** takes you to the game's full list, with filters and sorting, now with shorter rows and with dates and percentages written in Spanish.
- **The Achievements tab, more compact and without the look of separate windows.** The header takes a single line, with "Update achievements" and "Accounts and settings" inside; games and achievements are two cards with short rows, each game's icon instead of its cover, and no empty columns or lines.
- **More text in Spanish** in Achievements: games, progress, friends, showcase and its stats, menus and filters.

Logros más a mano en la ficha de cada juego, y una pestaña de Logros más compacta.
