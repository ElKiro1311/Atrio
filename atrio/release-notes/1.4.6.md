Notifications at hand in the top bar.

- **The bell is always there.** With no notifications it's dimmed; when there's one, it lights up in the theme's color and shows how many there are.
- **They open in a window under the bell**, instead of a bar that came in from the right. It closes when you click outside or on the bell again.
- **When there are none, it says so**, and each notice shows whether it's an error.
