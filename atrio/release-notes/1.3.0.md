Full screen gets a new design: it now looks and feels like a modern console.

- **A front page with the game's art.** Recently Played, Favorites and Most Played show the game's art across the whole screen, with its logo and a row of covers below. The selected game always stays in the same place and the covers move under it.
- **A grid for the whole library.** "All" and searches use a grid that takes the whole width, on 21:9 and 32:9 screens too.
- **A new game page.** Image, logo, playtime, achievements, developer, genre and description on a single page.
- **Everything redesigned.** Menus, settings, on-screen keyboard, filters and notices use Atrio's colors and accent, with switches and a clear focus you can see from the couch.
- **Controller buttons show again.** They came out as plain letters; now they are our own drawings, for Xbox and PlayStation.
- **Choose a store.** If a game is in several stores, Atrio asks which one to play it from, just like on the desktop. Covers show the icons of their stores.
- **More comfortable.** Filters come in your language, the on-screen keyboard has ñ and the triggers jump five games along the row.

Prefer the grid in every list? Turn this off in Settings > Layout.
