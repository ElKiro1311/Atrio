Controllers are recognized even when Atrio starts with Windows.

- **Controllers that aren't Xbox ones.** Atrio looks for its list of controllers in its own folder. Before, it looked in the folder it was opened from, and when starting with Windows (which opens programs from System32) it didn't find it, so some PlayStation, Switch or generic controllers might not be recognized in Couch Mode.

Los mandos se reconocen también cuando Atrio arranca con Windows.

- **Mandos que no son de Xbox.** Atrio busca su lista de mandos en su propia carpeta.
