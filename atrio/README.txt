ATRIO
=====

One place for the games of all your stores. It's portable: it isn't installed, it doesn't write to the registry and it
keeps everything inside this folder. To take it with you, copy the folder. To remove it, delete it.

Open Atrio.exe to start. (Spanish: LEEME.txt)


First start
-----------
If you have Playnite, Atrio offers to copy your library, your images and your store sessions. Playnite isn't touched and
the two can live together.

Windows may say that it doesn't recognize the program, because it isn't signed.


Updates
-------
Atrio lets you know when there's a new version, shows what's new and installs it with one click. No account is needed.
Your library and your settings aren't touched.
